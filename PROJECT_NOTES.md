# Project notes

## Portfolio framing

- Role: Product Designer
- Platform: Desktop web application
- Duration: Four-week concept sprint
- Audience: Industrial sustainability and operations teams
- Contribution: Research framing, information architecture, interaction design, visual design, UX writing, and measurement strategy

## Suggested usability tasks

1. Find the plant with the most urgent avoidable-energy anomaly.
2. Explain what happened and how confident the system is in the leading hypothesis.
3. Create a safe action plan and identify its accountable owner.
4. Confirm that production constraints were considered before implementation.
5. Decide whether the measured result justifies testing the intervention elsewhere.

## Success criteria for a future test

- At least 80% unassisted task completion.
- Priority anomaly identified within 30 seconds.
- Participants distinguish evidence strength from causal certainty.
- Participants distinguish observed reduction from annualized projections.
- No participant believes individual employee behavior is being monitored.
